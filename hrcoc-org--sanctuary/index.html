New here?

Here's exactly what your first Sunday looks like.

No pressure, no spotlight. We're a small, welcoming family — come as you are, and here's exactly what to expect on a Sunday.

1

Easy to find

We meet in the Aspen Room at the Hilton Garden Inn, 1050 Plaza Dr. — free parking and easy access. Watch for the welcome signs.

2

Walk right in

A friendly face will greet you and help you find the Aspen Room and a seat — that's it.

3

Bible class for all ages

Bible study for every age starts at 9:00 AM, before worship — a relaxed way to dig into Scripture together.

4

About an hour

Worship at 10:00 AM includes singing, the Lord's Supper, and plain preaching from the Bible — then stay for fellowship.
