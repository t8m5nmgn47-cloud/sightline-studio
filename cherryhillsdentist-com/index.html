Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (303) 757-8446 — a real person answers.

What services do you offer?

General dentistry with preventive care and restorative treatments; dental implants; Invisalign; cosmetic dentistry including whitening and veneers; TMD and pain management; and emergency care for severe toothaches and broken teeth.

Who are the doctors?

Dr. Bryan Gibbs specializes in advanced restorative and cosmetic dentistry and is a Fellow of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists, named to 5280 magazine's Top Doctors annually since 2010. Dr. Ben Bass combines state-of-the-art clinical techniques with a patient-first approach and earned his Doctor of Dental Medicine from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas in 2015.

What are your office hours?

Monday–Thursday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM; Friday, 8:00 AM–3:00 PM; closed Saturday and Sunday.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes, we are accepting new patients.
