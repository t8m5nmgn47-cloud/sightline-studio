Common questions

Know what comes next.

The team can address transaction-specific requirements directly. These are the questions a clearer website should help frame.

When should a title and escrow file be opened?

Timing depends on the transaction. Once the parties and contract details are ready, the title and escrow team can explain the information needed to establish the file and begin coordination.

Who will receive status updates?

Communication needs vary by transaction. A strong process establishes the right contacts and keeps the relevant parties informed as requirements and milestones move forward.

What should I bring to closing?

The exact requirements depend on the transaction and signing method. The closing team should provide specific instructions ahead of the appointment so there are no surprises.
