Common questions

Before we talk.

A first conversation should answer whether the relationship and working model are the right fit.

What is fractional general counsel?

An ongoing counsel relationship that gives a business access to strategic legal guidance without creating a full internal legal department. The exact scope depends on the company and its needs.

Is this only for urgent legal problems?

The model is most valuable when counsel is involved before every issue becomes urgent. Ongoing context helps leadership evaluate risk and options earlier.

Can legal guidance align with exit planning?

Long-term transition or exit goals can affect contracts, governance, risk, and operating decisions. Ongoing counsel can help keep those decisions aligned with the bigger direction.
