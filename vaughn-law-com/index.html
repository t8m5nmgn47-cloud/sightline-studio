Good to know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Reach out — a real person answers.

What size businesses do you work with?

We specialize in closely held businesses valued between $5 million and $100 million in revenue or enterprise value. This lower middle market is where thoughtful preparation and hands-on guidance can materially influence transaction outcomes.

What does fractional general counsel mean?

We provide ongoing strategic, legal, and operational guidance to select businesses. This work is often closely aligned with long-term exit planning, helping owners build and position their companies for future transactions.

Do you handle the entire M&A process?

Yes. We serve as lead sell-side M&A advisor throughout the process, guiding owners through preparation, positioning, and successful transition from early planning through closing.

What industries do you serve?

Our practice spans aerospace, manufacturing, retail, IT, nonprofits, banking, construction, real estate, franchisees, insurance carriers, accountants, physicians, wealth advisers, automotive, restaurants, and government contractors.

What is your background?

Justin Vaughn is a 32-year attorney who spent the first 20 years focused on litigation, maintaining an undefeated record in civil jury trials from 1994 to 2014. This disciplined, practical perspective guides his approach to complex negotiations and high-stakes business decisions.
