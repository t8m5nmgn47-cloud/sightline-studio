Our story

Built on Litigation Discipline, Focused on Exit Value

Justin Vaughn spent 20 years as a trial attorney with an undefeated civil jury record, then shifted to advising business owners on growth and exits. He has led 30+ acquisitions ranging from $5M to $700M+, serving as fractional general counsel to hundreds of companies and lead M&A advisor to closely held businesses in the lower middle market. His approach is business-minded and relationship-driven: he works with owners to align objectives, strengthen operations, and position companies for successful transitions.
