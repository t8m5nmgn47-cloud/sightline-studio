New here?

Here's exactly what your first Saturday looks like.

No pressure, no spotlight. We gather in homes, so come as you are, grab a seat, and stay as long as you like. Here's the plan so nothing's a surprise.

1

Find the gathering

We meet in homes across Castle Rock and Parker. Reach out and we'll send you the address and directions before Saturday.

2

Walk right in

A friendly host will greet you at the door and help you feel at home — that's it, no formalities.

3

Kids are covered

Our Children's Ministry runs during the gathering for kids from birth through 5th grade, with caring, trusted volunteers.

4

Saturdays at 5pm

Worship, prayer, and a down-to-earth message together — then time to share life and connect over dinner.

What we believe

A safe, welcoming place — in every way.

We believe church should feel like home. Here's what that looks like in practice.

✝

Grace, not performance

No dress code, no perfect life required. You're welcome exactly as you are — doubts, questions, and all.

🔒

Secure kids check-in & giving

Every child has a secure, background-verified check-in. Online giving is encrypted and handled by a trusted platform.

🤝

Genuinely inclusive

Real diversity of age, background, and story. We work hard to make sure no one feels invisible or out of place here.
