Our story

Holistic, Collaborative Care for Every Stage

South Denver OB/GYN & Midwives provides comprehensive women's health from adolescence through menopause across two locations serving the Denver metro, Parker, and Lone Tree areas. Our team of eight physicians, six Certified Nurse-Midwives, and four nurse practitioners brings diverse backgrounds and specialties to your care. We take a holistic, personal approach—building lasting relationships and coordinating obstetrics, gynecology, and infertility care as one team.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (303) 738-1100 — a real person answers.

What happens at an annual exam?

Your yearly well-woman check-up includes lab work. Most insurance covers the wellness exam in full each year.

Do you offer midwifery services?

Yes. Our Certified Nurse-Midwives provide full-scope care including pregnancy, well-woman physicals, gynecology, family planning, preconception counseling, and infertility consults. Midwifery services are available at our Castle Rock location.

What birth control options do you provide?

We offer birth control counseling and in-office procedures including IUDs and Nexplanon, performed by our physicians.

Do you treat urinary incontinence and pelvic floor problems?

Yes. Our uro-gynecology specialists provide expert evaluation of urinary and pelvic problems such as incontinence, with both surgical and non-surgical options.

What is bio-identical hormone replacement therapy?

We offer bio-identical hormone replacement using the pellet method to address symptoms of perimenopause and menopause.

Do you offer telemedicine visits?

Yes. We provide virtual care by phone or computer for hormone consults, weight-loss follow-ups, lab results, and other services.

Coverage

Insurance, simplified.

We bill in-network insurance and collect copays, coinsurance, and deductibles at the time of service. Self-pay patients receive transparent, affordable pricing. Payment plans available for balances; we accept cash, check, card, FSA, and HSA.
