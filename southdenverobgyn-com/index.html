Our story

Depth of expertise, personal approach

South Denver OB/GYN & Midwives brings together eight highly experienced physicians, six Certified Nurse-Midwives, and four Nurse Practitioners across Littleton and Castle Rock. We provide preventative health care and surgical care for women across all life stages. Our collaborative model means you have access to diverse specialties—from obstetrics and gynecology to uro-gynecology and infertility—without leaving the practice.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance card and driver's license. We will verify your insurance in advance and collect any copay, coinsurance, or deductible due at the time of service. If you have paper or electronic copies of your medical records, bring those as well, or we can help you request them from your previous provider.

Do you offer aesthetic services?

Yes. We partner with Allergan Aesthetics to offer facial rejuvenation treatments including BOTOX and JUVÉDERM, delivered by certified injectors (Caitlan Talamas, CNM; Karen Hashemi, CNM; and Maggie Rogers, RN). These services are available to help improve fine lines, wrinkles, facial asymmetry, and volume loss.
