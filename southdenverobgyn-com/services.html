Our services

The care you need, when you actually need it.

Primary care for well and sick patients of every age, with the visits, labs and follow-up handled under one roof.

Request an appointment →
01

Annual Exam

A yearly well-woman check-up and its lab work — most insurance covers the wellness exam in full each year.

02

Pregnancy

Physicians and nurse-midwives care for you as one team, with hospital birth at Littleton and Castle Rock Adventist Hospitals.

03

Midwifery

Certified Nurse-Midwives provide full-scope care: pregnancy, well-woman physicals and gynecology.

04

Birth Control

Birth control counseling and in-office procedures, including IUDs and Nexplanon, done by our physicians.

05

Gynecologic Surgery

Our physicians are trained in surgical procedures for a wide range of women's health issues, many done as outpatient care.

06

Uro-Gynecology

Expert evaluation of urinary and pelvic problems such as incontinence, with surgical and non-surgical options.

07

Hormone Therapy

Bio-identical hormone replacement using the pellet method, for symptoms of perimenopause and menopause.

08

Virtual Care

Telemedicine visits by phone or computer for hormone consults, weight-loss follow-ups, lab results and more.
