Good to know

Questions, answered.

What are your ratios and group sizes?

We keep ratios low — at or better than state requirements — so every child gets real attention. Exact numbers by age group are shared on your tour.

How do you handle security?

Secure entry, verified pick-up lists, and staff who personally know every family. Nobody unfamiliar walks in, and nobody unauthorized walks out.

Do you provide meals and snacks?

We'll walk you through our food program on your tour, including how we handle allergies — every classroom knows every child's needs.

How do I enroll?

Start with a tour — kids welcome. If it feels right, we'll walk you through availability, paperwork, and a gentle first-week transition plan.
