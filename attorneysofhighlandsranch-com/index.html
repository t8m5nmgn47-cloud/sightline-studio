Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. We offer free phone consultations to discuss your case and answer initial questions.

What areas of Colorado do you serve?

We represent clients throughout the Front Range, including Arapahoe, Denver, Douglas, Jefferson, Elbert, and El Paso counties. This includes Denver, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Castle Rock, Colorado Springs, and surrounding communities.

Do you offer mediation services?

Yes. Heidi Town provides mediation services throughout the Denver and Colorado Springs metropolitan areas. She will travel to your location at your convenience at no additional charge.

What payment options are available?

We offer flexible payment options for certain cases. Contact us at 720-344-5168 to discuss arrangements that work for your situation.

What types of criminal cases do you handle?

We defend all types of criminal cases, including DUI and DWAI, domestic violence, juvenile offenses, drug offenses, and assaults.

What family law matters do you handle?

We represent clients in divorce, parentage, child custody, property division, and spousal support modifications.
