Good to know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (720) 344-5168 — a real person answers.

What areas of law does Town & Town handle?

Divorce and family law (custody, property division, spousal support modifications), criminal defense (adult and juvenile), DUI/DWAI, domestic violence, restraining orders, juvenile delinquency, and estate planning.

Where do you serve clients?

The entire Front Range of Colorado, including Arapahoe, Denver, Douglas, Elbert, El Paso, and Jefferson counties. This covers Denver, Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Centennial, Englewood, Lakewood, Golden, Aurora, Parker, Castle Rock, Monument, Colorado Springs, and surrounding communities.

Does Town & Town offer mediation services?

Yes. Heidi Town provides mediation services throughout the Denver and Colorado Springs metropolitan areas. She travels to your office at the parties' convenience at no additional charge and works with your schedule to find the best time and location.

What payment options are available?

Town & Town offers flexible payment options for certain cases. Contact the firm at 720-344-5168 to discuss your specific situation.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Town & Town offers free phone consultations. Call 720-344-5168 to speak with an attorney.

What is Jeff Town's background?

Jeff Town has been licensed since 2003 and is a Colorado native who earned his law degree from the University of Colorado School of Law. He has first-chair litigation experience in jury trials for serious felonies and violent crimes, as well as civil jury trials. He is licensed to practice in all state and federal courts in Colorado and is a member of the Colorado Bar Association, Colorado Criminal Defense Bar, and Douglas and Elbert County Bar Association.
