Good to know

Questions, answered.

Why use an independent agent instead of buying online?

We compare multiple carriers for your situation and re-shop at renewal — the same coverage often costs less, and you get an advocate at claim time.

Will you review my current policy for free?

Yes — send it over and we'll flag gaps, overlaps, and savings, no obligation. Most people are surprised by what they find.

What happens when I need to file a claim?

Call us first. We'll walk you through it, deal with the carrier, and keep it moving — you're never on your own with an 800 number.

Can you bundle home and auto?

Usually, and it's often the fastest savings available. We'll run the numbers both ways and show you.
