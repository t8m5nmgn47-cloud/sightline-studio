# Portfolio rebuild v1

## Objective

Raise the public concept portfolio to the same product quality standard as Sightline's admin and intelligence experiences.

## First showcase batch

- The Vaughn Law Offices: editorial strategic-counsel positioning.
- Colorado Title & Escrow: transaction clarity and a visible closing journey.
- Aspen Falls Landscape & Construction: immersive design-build storytelling.
- Castle Rock CPA: accounting clarity, tax planning, and advisory positioning.

## Quality rules

- Every concept needs a specific title and meaningful meta description.
- Exactly one clear H1 promise per page.
- No placeholder business names, generic site titles, invented awards, fake reviews, fake client counts, or unsupported performance claims.
- Every site must be responsive, accessible by keyboard, and structured around a clear visitor decision path.
- Reuse design primitives, not visual identities: concepts in different verticals should not look like recolored clones.
- Calls to action must be visible and purposeful.

## Rollout

Validate the four showcase sites first, then rebuild the remaining gallery concepts in vertical batches. Expand the portfolio QA list as each batch is completed.
