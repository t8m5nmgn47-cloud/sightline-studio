Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can you help if I'm behind on my taxes?

Yes — catching up back years is routine work for us. We'll get you current, deal with any notices, and set you up so it doesn't happen again.

What do you charge?

Most of our work is flat-fee, quoted up front after a short conversation about your situation. No hourly meter running while you talk to us.

Do you work with small businesses?

They're the heart of our practice — bookkeeping, payroll, entity strategy, and the tax planning that keeps more profit in the business.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Last year's return and your questions. That's enough for us to spot what your current setup is costing you.
