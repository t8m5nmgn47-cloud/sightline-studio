Good to know

Questions, answered.

What does title insurance actually protect me from?

Hidden defects in the property's history — unknown liens, forged deeds, missed heirs. One premium at closing protects your ownership for as long as you hold the property.

How long does a closing take?

Most residential closings run 30–45 days from contract; refinances are often faster. We'll give you a timeline up front and flag anything that could move it.

How do you protect against wire fraud?

Verified instructions, calls to known numbers before any transfer, and staff trained on the latest schemes. Never wire from an email alone — we'll walk you through the safe process.

Can I choose my own title company?

In most transactions, yes — it's negotiable in the contract. Ask your agent to name us and we'll take it from there.
