Our story

Specialized care for nose, sinus, and airway

Dr. Arvin Rao founded South Denver ENT & Allergy in 2006 with a focus on comprehensive medical and surgical care of the ear, nose, and throat. His subspecialty training in rhinology and airway disorders informs treatment of chronic sinusitis, nasal obstruction, allergies, sleep apnea, and laryngopharyngeal reflux. The practice offers in-office procedures including balloon sinuplasty, VivAer, and RhinAer—minimally invasive options that address breathing and sinus problems without extended recovery.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (303) 953-6767 — a real person answers.

What is balloon sinuplasty?

Balloon sinuplasty is an endoscopic surgical technique using a catheter-based treatment for people who suffer from chronic sinusitis. It is performed in the office and offers a minimally invasive alternative to traditional sinus surgery.

What conditions does Dr. Rao treat?

Dr. Rao treats ear, nose, and throat conditions in both children and adults, including nasal obstruction, allergies, sinusitis, sleep apnea, tonsil and adenoid issues, voice disorders, swallowing disorders, hearing loss, tinnitus, dizziness, nosebleeds, and laryngopharyngeal reflux.

What is laryngopharyngeal reflux (LPR)?

Laryngopharyngeal reflux occurs when acid from the stomach backs up into the esophagus and pharynx. Dr. Rao has subspecialty training in diagnosing and treating this condition.

Does the practice offer pediatric care?

Yes. South Denver ENT & Allergy provides comprehensive medical and surgical care for both children and adults.
