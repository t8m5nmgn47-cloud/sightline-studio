Our story

Comprehensive ear, nose, and throat care for Denver.

South Denver ENT & Allergy, founded in 2006, is led by Dr. Arvin K. Rao, a board-certified otolaryngologist with specialized training in nasal, sinus, and upper respiratory disorders. The practice offers both medical and surgical care for children and adults, from routine allergies to complex sinus conditions. Dr. Rao works alongside physician assistants Shelby Conway and Carrie Chanos to provide personalized treatment and follow-up care designed to resolve problems and improve health.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is Balloon Sinuplasty?

Balloon Sinuplasty is an endoscopic surgical technique—a catheter-based treatment for people who suffer from chronic sinusitis. It is performed in our office.

What conditions do you treat?

South Denver ENT treats the full range of ear, nose, and throat conditions, including nasal allergies, sinusitis, nasal obstruction, sleep apnea, airway reflux, voice disorders, hearing loss, ear infections, and pediatric ENT care.

Does Dr. Rao see children?

Yes. South Denver ENT provides comprehensive medical and surgical care of the ear, nose, and throat in both children and adults.

What is airway reflux?

Laryngopharyngeal reflux, or LPR, occurs when acid from the stomach backs up into the esophagus and pharynx.
