Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is MonaLisa Touch?

MonaLisa Touch is a fractional CO2 laser procedure performed in our office in less than 5 minutes with no downtime. It is hormone and chemical-free and stimulates collagen production to improve vaginal health. It is commonly used to treat vaginal atrophy, dryness, itching, burning, and pain during intercourse.

What is the Acessa Procedure?

The Acessa Procedure is a minimally invasive, same-day, outpatient laparoscopic procedure that destroys and shrinks uterine fibroid tissue. It significantly reduces symptoms like heavy bleeding, abdominal pain, and frequent urination, allowing fast recovery and return to normal activities—without hysterectomy.

Do you offer pregnancy care?

Yes. With two decades of experience in obstetrics and gynecology, our team provides the superior skill, experience, and empathy needed to instill confidence in mothers-to-be. We also handle high-risk pregnancies and offer short-notice appointments for patient concerns.

What birth control options are available?

We assist women in selecting the best birth control for their body and lifestyle. Options include intrauterine devices (IUDs), Nexplanon® implants, and permanent fallopian tube occlusion.

What services are offered for menopause?

We help smooth the transition through menopause using both hormone and natural therapies. We offer BioTE Medical hormone replacement therapy, which is a leader in precision and preventive medicine for optimizing hormones.

What are your office hours?

Monday 8 AM–5 PM, Tuesday 8 AM–6 PM, Wednesday 8 AM–6 PM, Thursday 8 AM–5 PM, Friday 8 AM–5 PM. We are closed Saturday and Sunday.
