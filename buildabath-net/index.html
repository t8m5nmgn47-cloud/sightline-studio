Good to know

Questions, answered.

Don't see yours? Call (720) 742-1803 — a real person answers.

What makes Build A Bath different from other bathroom contractors?

We work from the studs up to address any rot or mold before starting your remodel. We don't use shower or tub overlays or liners. You get a design team, not a sales team. Half our team has over 30 years of tile experience each.

Do you offer a free consultation?

Yes. We offer a free design consultation. Call (720) 742-1803 or book online to schedule.

How long does a bathroom remodel typically take?

Projects vary by scope. Recent clients report completions in 2 weeks for full master bath remodels, though timelines depend on your specific project. We provide a detailed timeline during your consultation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Denver Metro area, including Littleton, Highlands Ranch, Parker, Lakewood, Aurora, Centennial, Thornton, Broomfield, Denver, and surrounding areas.

Do you handle all the work, or do you use subcontractors?

Our team includes master carpenters, plumbers, and electricians who coordinate every phase. You work directly with our team, not multiple subcontractors.

What warranty do your materials come with?

We source products with manufacturer warranties ranging from 10 years to lifetime coverage. All installations are backed by our satisfaction guarantee.
