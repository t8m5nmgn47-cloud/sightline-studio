Good to know

Questions, answered.

How much does an initial consultation cost?

Your initial consultation is free. We'll listen, give you an honest read on your situation, and outline your options — no obligation.

How are your fees structured?

It depends on the matter — some cases are flat-fee, some hourly, some contingency. You'll get the structure and an estimate in writing before we start.

How long will my case take?

Every matter is different, but after our first meeting we'll give you a realistic timeline and keep you updated at every stage — you'll never wonder what's happening with your case.

Will you actually take my case to trial if needed?

Yes. We prepare every case as if it's going to trial — which is exactly why most of them settle well.
