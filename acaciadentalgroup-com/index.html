Good to know

Questions, answered.

Are you taking new patients?

Yes — new patients are welcome and we hold same-week slots for first visits. Book online or call and we'll find a time that fits.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major dental plans and will verify your benefits before your visit, so there are no surprises. No insurance? Ask about our flexible payment options.

I haven't been to a dentist in years. Will that be a problem?

Not at all — you'll get a warm welcome, not a lecture. We'll take stock of where things stand and build a plan at whatever pace works for you.

What should I expect at my first appointment?

A conversation first, then a thorough exam and any needed X-rays. You'll leave knowing exactly where your oral health stands and what (if anything) comes next.
