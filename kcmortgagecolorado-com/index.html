Questions, answered.

What loan types are available for manufactured homes?

FHA loans with accessible terms for first-time buyers, VA loans offering zero down payment for eligible veterans, USDA loans for rural and suburban properties, and conventional loans for borrowers with strong financial profiles. Each program has different requirements, and we help you find the right fit.

When should I refinance my mortgage?

It's generally a good time to refinance when mortgage rates are 2% lower than your current rate. Even a 1% reduction can trim your monthly payments—for example, lowering a $100,000 loan from 8.5% to 7.5% saves $70 per month. Savings depend on your loan amount, term remaining, and new rate. We can calculate your specific options.

The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) reflects the total yearly cost of your mortgage, including the interest rate plus points, fees, loan-processing charges, underwriting, and private mortgage insurance. What is a point and should I pay points to lower my rate?

One point equals 1% of your loan amount. Discount points are fees you pay upfront to lower your interest rate. If you plan to stay in the property for at least a few years, paying points can reduce your monthly payment and may allow you to borrow more. If you plan to leave within one or two years, your monthly savings may not offset the upfront cost.

Can I get financing for a manufactured home on land I own?

Yes. If your manufactured home is permanently affixed to a foundation and you own the land, it's classified as real property and qualifies for traditional mortgage options including FHA, VA, USDA, and conventional loans.
