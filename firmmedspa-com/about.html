Our story

Founded on honest guidance

The Firm Medspa was founded in 2020 by Julie King after experiencing complications from cosmetic surgery. That experience shaped a philosophy: patients deserve honest recommendations based on what's truly in their best interest, whether that's a non-surgical solution or surgery. We combine advanced aesthetic, wellness, and regenerative therapies into personalized treatment plans designed specifically for you.
