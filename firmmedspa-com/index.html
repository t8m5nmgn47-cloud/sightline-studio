Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is EMFACE and how does it work?

EMFACE combines HIFES and RF energy to tone cheek and forehead muscles, improving brow position and jawline definition without surgery.

What can laser treatments address?

Laser resurfacing lessens fine lines, loss of skin tone, and improves overall complexion. The Firm uses Opus Plasma (microplasma and RF integration), ClearLift, and Dye VL for skin rejuvenation, tattoo removal, and pigment correction.

Do you offer weight loss treatments?

Yes. Our peptides and tirzepatide weight loss injections are available. Consultations are provided by our Physician Assistant with a focus on lifestyle modifications alongside injectable treatments.

Who performs injectables?

Licensed estheticians and physician assistant certified in neurotoxins and dermal fillers administer Botox, dermal fillers, and lip fillers. Dr. Edward Cobb, our medical director, supervises all procedures.

What is the Complexion Couture approach?

Each visit combines a uniquely curated range of protocols—facials, peels, lasers, injectables—designed to work together for your specific skin and body concerns, rather than single-service treatments.

What are your office hours?

Monday–Friday, 10am to 5pm. Other times are available by appointment.
