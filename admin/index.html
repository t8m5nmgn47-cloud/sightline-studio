How to launch a new prospect site (start to finish)
1

Generate the demo — no code needed

Double-click Sightline Site Maker.command in your sightline-studio folder. Type the prospect's website, click Generate site, check the quality grade, preview, then Publish. Live at /demos/<their-name>/ two minutes later.

2

Want the full funnel instead? (advanced)

Add the business to top30.json, run ./release.sh (regenerates demo, teardown, funnel + QA gate), then ./deploy.sh "added <business>". Live at /p/<slug>/.

3

Send it

Grab their funnel link from the Pipeline (Station 3) — every prospect has a ready-to-send email.

4

When they buy

Add them under Subscribers (Station 5), run Onboarding (Station 4), switch on their add-ons, point their domain, install legal pages.
