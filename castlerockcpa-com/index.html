Good to know

Questions before the first conversation.

Can we start with a specific accounting or tax need?

Yes. The first conversation should identify the immediate need, the condition of the current financial records, and what kind of ongoing support would be useful.

What information should I bring?

Bring the context behind the question. Depending on the work, the team can tell you which financial records, prior filings, or business documents are needed next.

What does advisory mean in practice?

Advisory work connects financial information to business decisions. The scope depends on the company and the questions leadership is trying to answer.
