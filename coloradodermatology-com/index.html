What makes Mohs surgery different from other skin cancer treatments?

Mohs micrographic surgery uses detailed mapping and complete microscopic examination of all removed tissue. This allows surgeons to pinpoint and remove even microscopic cancer roots invisible to the naked eye, resulting in the highest cure rate and removal of the least amount of normal skin.

Who performs Mohs surgery at Colorado Dermatology?

Dr. Matthew Mahlberg and Dr. Scott Mahlberg are both fellowship-trained dermatologic surgeons. Combined, they have performed over 20,000 Mohs surgery cases.

What skin conditions do you treat?

We treat skin cancer (basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, melanoma), acne, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, warts, hives, keloids, sun damage, and many other conditions. We also offer cosmetic treatments including injectables, laser therapy, and skin rejuvenation procedures.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes. We accept a wide range of insurance plans including Medicare, commercial plans, and government medical coverage. Our appointment form allows you to specify your insurance details.

Are there clinical trial opportunities?

Yes. We currently have active clinical trials enrolling at our Denver location. Participants receive treatment from a board-certified dermatologist at no cost while contributing to medical research.

Why is skin cancer screening important in Colorado?

Colorado's high altitude and outdoor-active lifestyle increase skin cancer risk. A full-skin exam with our dermatologists can detect spots of concern early and provide education on risk reduction.
