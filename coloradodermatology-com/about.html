Our story

Leaders in Dermatology and Mohs Surgery

Colorado Center for Dermatology & Skin Surgery combines surgical expertise with medical and cosmetic dermatology across three locations. Our team of seven board-certified physicians, led by fellowship-trained Mohs surgeons Dr. Matthew Mahlberg and Dr. Scott Mahlberg, has performed over 20,000 Mohs surgery cases combined. We deliver personalized care grounded in a mission to restore hope in healthcare through deep expertise and a help-first mentality.
